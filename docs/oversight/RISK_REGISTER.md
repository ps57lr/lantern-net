## Immediate conditions and ownership

1. **Program lead:** keep implementation inside the dedicated worktree; preserve v0.2.1 as the comparison point; record scope and clean validation evidence.
2. **Core/Platform:** fix or explicitly schedule R-012, R-013, R-015, and R-016 before diagnosis can authorize actions.
3. **Core/Privacy:** build sensitivity-aware evidence/report contracts and canary tests before automatic report transport.
4. **Product/UI:** produce consent, state, risk, accessibility, responsive technician, and rescue interaction specifications; show unsupported/denied/inconclusive states honestly.
5. **Security/Session:** complete LAN and local-web ADR/threat update before opening a non-loopback port.
6. **Remediation:** remain dry-run/mock only until R-001 through R-003 and R-019 have executable test evidence.
7. **Recovery:** remain guidance/read-only only until R-009 and R-024 receive platform-specific review.
8. **Release:** do not push, tag, sign, notarize, publish, or enable updates without explicit user authorization.

## Phase verdict criteria

- **PASS:** all risks gating that phase have implemented controls and reviewed evidence; residual risks are accurately documented.
- **PASS WITH CONDITIONS:** safe work may continue, but named controls must land before the next trust boundary is crossed.
- **BLOCK:** a critical boundary is crossed without its controls, tests are weakened to fit behavior, credentials/secrets enter artifacts, real mutation occurs without approval, or the UI materially overstates coverage/safety.

Current verdict: **PASS WITH CONDITIONS**. Work may continue on Phase 1 contracts and read-only UI foundations. Remediation execution, LAN exposure, credential handling, and rescue writes remain blocked.
